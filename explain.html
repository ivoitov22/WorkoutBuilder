The four hovers are as follows
1. The zoom on the exercise card on hover
2. The box-shadow on the exercise card on hover
3. The cursor style change on the exercise card on hover
4. The green border color change whenever you hover over the workout canvas
While all of the cards all do have the same hover event and the events are stacked onto these cards, there are 3 distinct hover effects, and I felt that adding in elements just to have 4 different elements with 4 different hovers would look clunky in this context and take away from the essence of the project. So I stacked unique hovers onto the same element.
In the case that the three different hovers on the exercises cards dont count as unique since they are stacked on the same element, then the four would be the cursor change on the card exercise hover, the color change to green on the canvas dotted lined, the opacity change when you hover over the search bar, and the close button on the detail popout has a zoom effect on hover


The six unique movements are as follows
1-4. The four different elements moving in from different sides when a card is double clicked, with the close button going from right to left, the description from bottom to top, the image from top to bottom, and the notes box from left to right. Also all four of these elements go the opposite direction when the box is closed
5. The inclusion of the drag and drop feature counts as one movement, since each element can be moved back and forth
6. The ability to order the items on each side by dragging them above or below eachother is another type of movement, however if this doesn't count then there is a fixed sidebar element that also moves out from the side and then back in.


Media screens are self-explanatory with one change at 1200px and one at 1000px
